      Voice Lab
      
        
      Test how well Jarvis hears you. Recordings stay in this window; nothing is saved. "Hey Jarvis" is switched off for real while this page is open.
      
        
      ·
      
        
      
          
      1. Microphone check
      
          
      Two seconds of silence, then three seconds of you talking. Tells you if the microphone is too quiet, too loud, or the room too noisy.
      
          
       
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      2. Speech test
      
          
      Say each sentence and press Stop. Every engine on this PC (and Groq, if switched on) tries to write it down; the page shows how many words were wrong.
      
          
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      3. Wake word test
      
          
      Record "Hey Jarvis" a few times (about 4 to 5, with different tones), then one or two clips of ordinary talk. The page tries the three sensitivities and suggests the best for your voice.

      Setup guide
      
        
      Jarvis listens to you, answers aloud and can act on this PC. Everything below is optional and can be done later from the tray menu. Nothing leaves this PC unless you add an AI key or sign in to an account yourself.
      

      
      
        
      1. Microphone 
      
        
      Jarvis only listens while the blue ring shows (or, if you turn the wake word on, for the phrase "Hey Jarvis" only, on this PC). Check that Windows lets him use your microphone.
      
        
       
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      2. Voice 
      
        
      A natural voice and speech recognition that run on this PC (about 130 MB, downloaded once, each file checked before use). Without them Jarvis uses the Windows voice and Windows recognition.
      
        
       
      
        
      
      

      
      
        
      3. AI brain 
      
        
      To chat and to handle requests that are not simple commands, Jarvis needs a free AI key (Groq is the quickest, about a minute). Simple commands such as opening apps, volume, timers and music work without one.
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      4. Mail and calendar 
      
        
      Optional. Connect your own Google account so Jarvis can read your mail and calendar and send mail you approve.
      
        
      
      
      

      
       You can open this again from Settings → Setup.
